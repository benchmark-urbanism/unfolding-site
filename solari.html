Solari

One drum of flaps, printed with the alphabet and the digits. It can only turn forward. Tap it.

Scroll to read. Tap the board at any point.

A word

A row of drums spells a word. Each drum turns forward until it reaches its letter. A letter far round the drum takes longer, so the drums settle at different times.

The count at the top right is the number of flaps that have fallen.

The board

A departures board is rows of these drums, one for each character. When the departures change, the drums turn together. The noise makes people in the waiting room look up.

Designed by an architect

Solari di Udine made flip clocks and boards in the north-east of Italy. The architect Gino Valle designed its Cifra 5 clock (Compasso d'Oro, 1956) and its boards for stations and airports (Compasso d'Oro, 1962).

His Cifra 3 clock of 1966, with lettering by Massimo Vignelli, is in the collection of the Museum of Modern Art.

Told

Each drum shows the character the board's controller sends it. A drum is as simple as Truchet's tile, and the controller decides what the board displays.
