The deck

After the girih tiles and after Carcassonne: six tiles on one square, a line on each road edge running to the middle, road meeting road and land meeting land across the joins.

After the Jacquard loom: a street held on a punched card, so that the card is edited and the street follows.

After the loom's threading, and after the Passacaglia's ground: the cards woven onto the district's streets, and the ground holding while the voices move.

After the loom's cloth and the fugue's voices: a row a tick, and three speeds answering each other, the walkers, the shops, and the cards.
