Jacquard

A card with holes in it, and a length of cloth. Punch a hole, and the cloth changes.

Scroll to read. Tap the cards at any point.

Warp and weft

A loom holds the warp, threads stretched along its length and fixed for the whole piece. The weft passes across it one row at a time. On each pass some warp threads are lifted and the weft runs beneath them. The warp shows where it is lifted, and the weft shows elsewhere.

In plain weave, one row lifts alternate threads and the next row lifts the rest.

The card

Joseph Marie Jacquard's loom of 1804 read which threads to lift from punched cards, taken in turn, one for each row of cloth. The places for holes are numbered across the card. Place 3 on each card is punch column 3. Each thread is mapped to one punch column. A hole punched in a card lifts the threads mapped to its column in each row that card weaves.

Plain weave needs two punch columns, one for the odd threads and one for the even, and two cards, one for each row. Twill needs at least three punch columns and satin at least five. Tap the dashed hole on a card to add a column, up to eight.

Hover over a hole, or tap one, to see the threads mapped to its column and the rows its card weaves. A tap also punches or fills it.

The board starts with one card of four punch columns. With a single card the rows are identical, so the threads a hole lifts run as stripes down the cloth. Tap the holes.

A chain of cards

The cards are laced into a chain, one card a row, and the chain loops. Four cards give a twill, the diagonal weave of denim.

Tap the dashed card to add another, up to eight. The Cards − button beneath the board takes one away.

The thread mapping

The numbers above the cloth give each thread's punch column. Any thread can be mapped to any column, and several threads, side by side or apart, can share one. In the Straight mapping the columns run 1, 2, 3, 4 and repeat across the width. In the Mirror mapping they run forwards and back, 1, 2, 3, 4, 3, 2, and the same four cards weave zigzags.

The mapping is made once, when the loom is set up, and it holds for the whole piece. On the loom each punch column is a hook, and the mapping is the harness, the cords that tie each thread to its hook. Tap a number to map that thread to the next column.

Lovelace

We may say most aptly that the Analytical Engine weaves algebraical patterns just as the Jacquard-loom weaves flowers and leaves.Ada Lovelace, note A, 1843

Charles Babbage meant his engine to be programmed with punched cards taken from the loom. He owned a portrait of Jacquard woven in silk in 1839 on a Jacquard loom, from some 24,000 cards.

Albers's draft

Anni Albers drafted before she wove. A draft writes the thread mapping along the top (weavers call it the threading), the cards down the side, and the cloth where they cross, in black and white squares on squared paper.

The board is now drawn as a draft. Change the cards or the mapping, and the cloth follows.
